## Three decisions before line edits

1. **Tú or usted.** The whole site currently uses *tú* ("Cuéntanos quién eres", "tus llamadas", "Pedir cotización" is neutral). Owners of HVAC, plumbing, law and dental businesses in the US are the audience; Remi's example call uses *usted* with the caller. Decide one register for the site and one for Remi, and apply it everywhere.
2. **Regional trade vocabulary.** Plomero vs fontanero; calefacción vs aire acondicionado vs climatización for HVAC; "techos" for roofing; "bufete" vs "despacho" for law firm; "sillón" for the dental chair. Pick the US-Spanish terms the target markets search for, not the Spain forms.
3. **Does "Que te reserven." carry the weight of "Get booked."?** The H1 is "Que te encuentren. Que te contesten. Que te reserven." "Reservar" reads as hotel or restaurant in some markets; "Que te agenden." is the alternative used elsewhere in the copy ("agendar", "cita"). Choose one and align the verb across H1, doors and CTAs.
